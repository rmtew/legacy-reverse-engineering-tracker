# Focused Amiga and C64 discovery — 54 approved projects

Added **54 primary-source-reviewed projects**, increasing the catalogue from **1,444 to 1,498**. Thirty-two are Amiga-related and twenty-four C64-related, with Silk Dust and Interword connecting both. Five reviewed holds remain non-promoted.

## Evidence, scope and verification

All 54 roots were screened against the fresh catalogue, source and decision indexes plus a 1,637-root prior-review union. Each addition has eight evidence-backed audit areas: identity, classification, source CPU, target CPU, build, runtime profiles, AI and relationships. Source graphs remain partial; unknown facts remain unknown.

No candidate project was compiled, run, played, emulated or byte-compared. Positive build/runtime fields are explicitly bounded upstream reports. Historical source availability does not establish a modern reproducible build, completeness or unrestricted asset rights.

Highlights include original Alien3, Word Writer 6, Interword, Dinozorus, Leonardo, Rolling Ronny, Snackzone and Brain Braiser source; AmiGalaga, ReFlood and Pinball Illusions reconstructions; native C64 arcade ports; R-PAGE, SDDDE, SAGE, C64 Studio, Reblitz64, LADS and Rog preservation. Four projects explicitly support PowerPC AmigaOS 4 development; they are not represented as classic 68k-compatible builds.

Important limits include Pinball Illusions’ eight unimplemented mission opcodes and deliberate rule divergence; LADS’ reported matching 4,986-byte prefix within a 5,032-byte output; Breathless’ unfilled license template; original ROM/data requirements for AmiGalaga and SMB1; recovered pre-final Interword source; and the incomplete NetBSD rump initialization and RTL8139 receive path.
