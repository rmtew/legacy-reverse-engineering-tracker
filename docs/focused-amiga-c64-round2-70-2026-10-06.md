# Focused Amiga/C64 discovery, second pass: 70 approved projects

Added 70 approved source-reviewed projects, taking the catalogue from 1,498 to 1,568. Seventeen holds, one audit-only hybrid game-specific runtime and nine lineage/reference decisions remain non-promoted.

## Scope and verification

The exact approved roots were screened against the fresh catalogue, source/decision indexes and a 1,699-root prior-review union. All 70 have eight evidence-backed audit areas: identity, classification, source CPU, target CPU, build, runtime profiles, AI and relationships. Wider source graphs remain partial.

No candidate was built, run, emulated, played or byte-compared. Build/runtime fields distinguish bounded upstream statements from independent checks; every byte_exact value remains unknown. Licenses and asset rights are recorded separately from source visibility. Native ISA, compiler optimization and verified runtime minimum are distinct.

Highlights include original Gloom, DeluxePaint, Habitat and Ambermoon archives; Stunt Car Racer disassembly; native AmiVikings2, NBlood, PCExhumed and Knightmare ports; browser Below the Root, Blagger and ACEBasicJS reconstructions; Supermon+64, UVK, Oberon-A and Amiga replayer analysis; C64 games and authoring tools.

Rescue on Fractalus is audit-only. The absence of an opcode-fetch loop did not remove its game-specific emulation: targeted bus.h inspection established POKEY RANDOM/register handling, GTIA sprite shadows, ANTIC display-list/DMA state and retained 64 KiB CPU-state semantics alongside native rewrites.

Original ACE and older GEOS archives remain non-counted predecessor provenance. Three historicalsource forks and a ZGloom macOS derivative are not counted again. Generic machine emulators are distinct from game-specific runtime wrappers.

Important caveats include DeluxePaint’s restrictive museum license, Gloom’s source-only public-domain grant, Habitat’s case-sensitive paths/missing tools, Ambermoon’s explicitly incomplete unlicensed snapshot, c64kit’s prototype game loop, original game/ROM data requirements, PAL-only claims and modern toolchain incompatibilities.
