### Identity (reviewed)

The original programmer identifies Totally as his unfinished-but-playable 1983 high-school side shooter. TOTALLY.S contains the actual player, missile, enemy, fuel/lives, collision, boss-stage and high-score state paths, rather than only a launcher.
- https://github.com/randall-frank/totally/blob/e2a3e383b5f8f24f0a40ef37689ee315e603f43c/README.rst
- https://github.com/randall-frank/totally/blob/e2a3e383b5f8f24f0a40ef37689ee315e603f43c/src/TOTALLY.S

### Classification (reviewed)

This is a source reconstruction from the author’s hand-entered machine code, assisted by SourceGen, not an intact original assembly listing. Unused gaps and patch chains were removed; DOS 3.3 raw-sector high scores were replaced with ProDOS file I/O; another 16 enemy images came from the author’s unfinished SEU project.
- https://github.com/randall-frank/totally/blob/e2a3e383b5f8f24f0a40ef37689ee315e603f43c/README.rst
- https://github.com/randall-frank/totally/blob/e2a3e383b5f8f24f0a40ef37689ee315e603f43c/src/TOTALLY.S
