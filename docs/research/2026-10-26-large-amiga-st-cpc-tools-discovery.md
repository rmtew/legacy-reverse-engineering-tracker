## Research event notes

Root assistant performed this discovery and publication personally, without a worker, PR or new branch. Nine fresh searches covered Amiga, Atari ST, CPC, BBC, Spectrum and Commodore sources and tools. Known entries and unrelated substring hits were screened before deeper inspection. Seven fork parents were verified against existing catalogue records. Hardware-specific source restorations, original homebrew, native remakes, educational projects and development tools are distinguished. Build limitations include missing assets, documented crashes, tokenized source, untested parsers and historical compiler requirements. No upstream code, bundled executable, game, ROM or disk image was run; source review is not independent build/runtime verification. Two connector-decoded non-UTF-8 texts are referenced by pinned URLs but deliberately excluded from inline proofs because their bytes could not be verified. Remaining proof blobs are hash-verified before sealing.
