All discovery and publication performed personally by the root assistant, without a research worker, PR or new branch. Five fresh platform searches were combined with previously unreviewed Atari/C64 search candidates and screened against current main. Thirty selected roots received immutable primary-source inspection, including implementation rather than README alone. Generic Oric/microbit substring hits were triaged out and are not counted as reviewed projects. Original MSX homebrew, incomplete demonstrations, historical application source, developer tools and modern reimplementations remain distinct. Explicit hardware requirements, guessed image ordering, missing source containers, parser robustness limitations and predecessor relationships are retained. No upstream program, game, ROM, disk image or test suite was executed; source review does not independently certify build or gameplay. Pinned proof blobs were verified locally before sealing.
