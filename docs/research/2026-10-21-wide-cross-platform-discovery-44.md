## Research event notes

Root personally ran 52 cross-platform searches yielding 564 hits and 560 distinct repository URLs, inspected 36 selected roots, and prepared 34 additions plus two explicit non-promotions. Search covered classic computers, consoles, handhelds, firmware, language/runtime reconstructions, source recovery, development tools, asset/audio/disk tools and binary analysis. Broad coverage is not exhaustion of every platform. No discovery worker used and no upstream software or game content executed. Current catalogues were refreshed from 48f2cbd2ff2e4dc2bd1fd108162a6be379901e85; activity history is an older cached file, so trusted-main live validation remains required. Ingestion findings: byte-exact builds may include unrecovered assembly (GX Spirit Caller and Sly); completed matching may retain handwritten assembly/toolchain patches and unresolved names (Parasite Eve II); a complete-firmware goal can conflict with explicit far-from-complete status (uOFW). Resolve platform collisions from implementation: Educational System Master Cartridge is Atari, CPM-udis86 is CMake, SVP is SSP160x rather than 68000, and a BBC-derived Lisp can target modern Linux. Treat decompiler names as claims: 68000Decompiler emits assembly listings, FPLAY processes sequence bytecode, Pandemonium publishes semantic notes rather than C. BASIC renumberer splits colons before protecting quoted strings, rewrites ON expressions to a placeholder and can remap targets twice, so blanket preservation claims are not ingested. N64 networking is PC-tethered; WonderSwan checksum generation is not validation; audio format/container support is not decoder equivalence. Missing README did not prevent source-based review. Oversized source returned empty without error, while a large recursive tree failed transport: the latter was recovered through commit-pinned file reads, and the former retained as an explicit inspection backlog rather than silently accepted or classified empty. Preserve AI review/demonstration scope and licence restrictions; no build flags promoted from prose alone.
