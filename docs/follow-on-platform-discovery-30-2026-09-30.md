# Follow-on platform discovery: 30 more candidates (2026-09-30)

Assessed 30 further distinct platform-focused leads: 28 projects promoted, one derivative converter excluded and one original-source archive identified as already-covered lineage.

This is the second separately requested batch of 30, following the earlier same-day fresh-platform pass. Its candidate URLs are disjoint from that first batch. Repository searches were screened locally against projects and the decision ledger before detailed primary-source inspection. The saved queue was reviewed; this pass intentionally sought additional Amiga, Atari ST, CPC and C64 leads. Search hits sometimes led to other original platforms or modern targets, recorded at their demonstrated scope.

## Important distinctions

- Knight Lore has a native, RAM-derived Z80 reconstruction. Main-branch documentation reports RASM assembly and snapshot boot to the original menu, which does not establish full playability or whole-binary byte equivalence. The repository default is a separate VRAM experiment; the reconstruction record pins main. Its browser room visualizer is not advertised as a complete playable port.
- Hard Drivin' source is labelled Atari ST upstream, but inspected assembly and hardware interfaces demonstrate DOS/x86. The record follows actual artifacts and leaves clean build, modern CPU and playable output unknown.
- Ghost Town analyzes the Commodore 16 original and ports to C64/Plus/4. Mutant Monty is an Amiga conversion. Source-game heritage is not automatically evidence of original-platform binary analysis.
- Mars Patrol qualifies through a documented creator-led disassembly/reassembly and released binaries, while public reconstructed source and build availability remain unknown. Lawn Job is author-restored BASIC, not a binary disassembly.
- Phantom Fighter preserves original Amiga source alongside directly implemented Pygame/JavaScript recreations; no game-specific guest CPU interpreter was found in those modern paths. Its conflicting 68000/68010 statements do not become a minimum hardware claim.
- Ninja Carnage is one multi-platform native project, not a separate catalogue entry per platform. Released CPC/C64 playability does not establish current Amiga source reproducibility.
- FujiBoink's six period source components already exist in the tracked conversion lineage; whitespace-normalized comparison confirms overlap. The original archive remains linked as a useful source, with no duplicate project.

## Discovery tooling improvement

The bounded inventory now recognizes .dams, .cs and .kt source files. DAMS uses its native assembler format; Airwolf and Fruity Frank supply C# and Kotlin implementations. This only reports a source-file hint and never determines eligibility or buildability. A regression test covers each suffix, case-insensitive matching, directories and misleading compound suffixes. No requests or scheduling changes are added.

Larger proposed improvements, not implemented in this pass:

- Surface likely original-platform/target-platform contradictions when primary source instruction sets disagree with README labels; keep the final decision evidence-led and manual.
